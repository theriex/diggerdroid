App name (30chars): Digger Collection Listening

Short description (80chars): Shuffle-style player with controls matching selection to your listening context.

Full description (4000chars):

Digger is a shuffle-style music library player featuring the controls you need to match your music to your listening context. Adjust the Energy Level range you want, whether you are listening in a flow state, what Approachability range you want, whether you are listening with others, and more. Mark up and adjust what's playing as you feel. Play your library.

Whenever you start Digger, it finds your least recently played artists, distributes your least recently played songs across those artists, puts those songs on deck, and plays. What's different about Digger is you can (as you want) adjust energy level, approachability, keywords, playback frequency, stars, and personal notes as you are listening. Any markup you add gets used in future retrieval, so your library becomes a personal reflection of you. Switch to album playback anytime. Fast find music by Artist, Album, Title, genre/keyword and personal notes simultaneously.

Digger is free and open source. If you want, you can sign in on DiggerHub (also free) to share your music impressions with others and across your own Digger instances.

It's called Digger because it helps you dig through the stacks.
